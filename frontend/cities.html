How Intercity Works

🚗 Can I book a ride from Lahore to Islamabad?

Yes! Select "Intercity" ride type, set your pickup in Lahore and destination in Islamabad. The app finds drivers making that trip.

💰 How is intercity price set?

You suggest a price. The app shows a guide based on distance. Drivers who can make the trip accept or counter-offer.

🕐 How early should I book?

For intercity, book at least 2–3 hours in advance. You can also schedule for tomorrow.

🧳 How many bags can I bring?

Mention luggage needs in your ride request. Drivers with enough space will respond.
